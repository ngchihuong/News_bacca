- (decision) Product direction: community-driven news social platform, not traditional CMS. Two content streams in single feed: community posts (priority) and journalist-tagged posts.
- (decision) Single feed with journalist badge/tag, no separate tab. Filter support for journalist-only content later.
- (decision) Content moderation: free posting with report system + automated content scanning for violations.
- (decision) Content visibility: users control their own posts (public/private/followers-only).
- (decision) Feature priority: Auth (foundation) -> Personal Feed -> UGC (user posts) -> Interactions (like/comment/share) -> Follow/Unfollow -> Journalist tags -> Ad improvements.
- (decision) Market: Vietnam first, global expansion later. Dual purpose: learning/technology exploration + real monetization.
- (event) Brief draft v1 written to brief.md. Addendum written to addendum.md with technical inventory, user role matrix, competitive landscape, and rejected alternatives.
- (assumption) Lotus (VN social network) closure cited as cold-start cautionary tale - assumed relevant comparison. Auth reordered before Feed in priority despite user listing it after - justified as technical dependency.
